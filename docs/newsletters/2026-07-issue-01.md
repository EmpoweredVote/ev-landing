## Body

Hi [First name],

I'm Chris Andrews, one of the volunteers at Empowered Vote — and since this is the first note we've ever sent, it feels right to start with a thank-you. You're one of a small handful who chipped in *before* there was much to point at, and that early trust is the only reason we've been able to keep building.

> **[YOUR STORY — 2–3 sentences. Chris writing this one.]**
> *What you were doing before, and the moment civic stuff clicked for you / why EV's "shared solutions" idea pulled you in. Plain and personal — this is the sincerity beat.*

**Why now:** we just crossed **under 100 days** until the general election — and several states still have primaries in the next few weeks. Our aim is simple: by November, any voter should be able to walk into the booth actually informed — whatever their party, their zip code, or how little time they have.

**How far we've come.** You might not realize how much is already real — the five tools on our site today:

- **Empowered Essentials** — type your zip and see every official who represents you, how to reach them, their record, and who funds their campaigns.
- **Empowered Compass** — map where you stand on the issues you care about, then see how candidates line up with you on the very same scale.
- **Read & Rank** — a blind taste test for candidates: read what they actually said with the names hidden, rank what you like, *then* see who said it.
- **Treasury Tracker** — city, state, and federal budgets, line by line, sourced to the penny (no best-guess numbers wearing a real-looking label).
- **Civic Trivia Championships** — a fast, friendly, head-to-head way to test how well you really know how government works.

Under the hood that's $4.7B in campaign contributions, every state's budget, and a researched official in all 50 states. [The full numbers live at **/briefing**.] And if you want the whole vision in one place, we just published an interactive [**Purpose mind map**] you can wander through.

We're still eight volunteers, still very much in alpha, and racing to get these in front of real voters before November. We'll send one of these a month — honest about what shipped and what's still rough.

**Our hardest problem, honestly, is reach** — plenty of people would use these tools if they knew they existed. So the single most helpful thing you can do: **forward this to one person who'd care.** A note from you beats anything we could buy.

Thanks for being here early.
— Chris *(and the rest of the volunteers)*

*P.S. If you're able to keep your monthly support going, it doesn't change what we build — just how fast we get there. And if now's not the time, that's completely okay; everything we make stays free, for everyone.* [**Chip in →**]
