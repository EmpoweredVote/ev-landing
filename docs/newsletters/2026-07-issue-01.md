# Newsletter — Issue #01 (July 2026)

*Draft · audience: the small list following the build — early donors **plus** newer newsletter sign-ups · sending via GiveButter · revised against the approved [voice-and-tone.md](../voice-and-tone.md) — its six principles (explain the idea, show your work, respect the reader, be transparent, own the mistake, build for the long term) and its "Before you publish" checklist*

Goal: a warm first note to the people following the build — some chipped in early,
some only just signed up, and a few are new enough that this is their first real look
at us. A shortened, human version of `/briefing`. Emotional core = gratitude + honest
progress. At this list size it should read like a personal note from Chris, not a
designed newsletter.

Deliberately **no personal story about Chris** — the note carries its weight through
what shipped and what's still rough, not through a founder-style origin tale. One
"look how far we've come" (the five tools), one honest "what's still rough" beat, and
**one ask: hit reply** (forwarding is the soft secondary).

Thank people for their **interest and support, financial or otherwise** — never assume
the reader has given money. No donation ask in issue #01: the donors already gave, and
an ask should not be a new subscriber's first contact with us. Timing hook: under 100
days to the general election. Target length ~2.4-min read (~490 words) — the body is now complete prose, no
placeholders, so that is a real count rather than a target.

## Body

Hi [First name],

I'm Chris Andrews, one of the volunteers at Empowered Vote. This is the first note we've ever sent, so it should start with a thank-you — to those of you who chipped in early, and to those of you who signed up to follow along. You're all here before there's much to point at, which is the harder time to show up.

**Why now:** we're under 100 days from the November general election, and some states still have primaries first. By November, any voter should be able to walk into the booth actually informed — whatever their party, their zip code, or how little time they have.

**What exists today** — five free tools, all live on the site:

- **Empowered Essentials** — type your zip and see who represents you: how to reach them, their record, who funds them.
- **Empowered Compass** — map where you stand on the issues, then see how candidates line up on the same scale.
- **Read & Rank** — a blind taste test for candidates: read what they said with the names hidden, rank what you like, *then* see who said it.
- **Treasury Tracker** — city, state, and federal budgets, line by line, traced back to the source documents.
- **Civic Trivia Championships** — a fast, friendly way to test how well you know how government works.

Under the hood: 6 million federal campaign contributions totaling $4.7B, each traceable to its original FEC filing; audited budget data for 2,435 governments across all 50 states; and researched officials in every state. [The full numbers live at **/briefing**.]

**Where it's still rough.** One caveat on that last one. Every state has at least one researched official, and we cover all 50 congressional delegations — but state and local depth is uneven: nine states go deep, three are partway, and in the other 38 it's mostly governors and statewide officers so far. Look up your city council in one of those 38 and you'll find gaps. Better you hear that from me.

We're still nine volunteers, still very much in alpha, and still filling those gaps ahead of November. We'll send one of these a month — what shipped, and what's still rough.

**One ask this month, and it's not money: hit reply.** Tell me what you'd want to see in these notes, or what confused you on the site. This list is still short enough that your reply comes straight to me, not into a queue.

And if you know someone who'd care, forward it along. Reach is our hardest problem — plenty of people would use these tools if they knew they existed. A note from you beats anything we could buy.

Thanks for being here early.
— Chris *(and the rest of the volunteers)*
