# Newsletter — Issue #01 (August 2026)

*Draft · audience: the small list following the build — early donors **plus** newer newsletter sign-ups · sending via GiveButter · revised against the approved [voice-and-tone.md](../voice-and-tone.md) — its six principles (explain the idea, show your work, respect the reader, be transparent, own the mistake, build for the long term) and its "Before you publish" checklist*

Goal: a warm first note to the people following the build — some chipped in early,
some only just signed up, and a few are new enough that this is their first real look
at us. A shortened, human version of `/briefing`. Emotional core = gratitude + honest
progress. At this list size it should read like a personal note from Chris, not a
designed newsletter.

Deliberately **no personal story about Chris** — the note carries its weight through
what shipped and what's still rough, not through a founder-style origin tale. The
body should show that the work is substantial without turning into a feature catalog,
then make the remaining gaps clear. **One ask: hit reply** (forwarding is the soft
secondary).

Thank people for their **interest and support, financial or otherwise** — never assume
the reader has given money. No donation ask in issue #01: the donors already gave, and
an ask should not be a new subscriber's first contact with us.

Timing hook: about 75 days to the general election (Tuesday, November 3, 2026). Say a
real number, not "under 100 days" — see the send-day checklist item. Target length
~2.6-min read (~525 words); the body is complete prose, so that is a real count
rather than a target.

## Body

Hi [First name],

I'm Chris Andrews, one of the volunteers at Empowered Vote. This is the first note we've ever sent, so I'll start with a thank-you — whether you chipped in early or signed up to follow along. You found us before there was much to see, and that matters to us. Your support, whether financial or otherwise, has helped get us to this point. It told us we weren't the only people who thought this mission was worth building, and gave us a reason to keep going while most of the work was still invisible.

The November general election is about 75 days away, and some states still have primaries first. We're working toward a simple standard: when someone is ready to vote, they should be able to find clear information about the people and institutions on their ballot.

**What we've built**

Here is what that looks like today:

- [Empowered Essentials](https://essentials.empowered.vote) shows you who represents you, how to contact them, what they have done, and who has funded them.
- [Empowered Compass](https://compass.empowered.vote) lets you map your priorities and compare them with candidates on the same scale.
- [Read & Rank](https://readrank.empowered.vote) hides the names and parties so you can read candidates' actual positions before deciding what you think.
- [Treasury Tracker](https://treasurytracker.empowered.vote) lets you follow city, state, and federal budgets back to their source documents.
- [Civic Trivia Championships](https://ctc.empowered.vote) gives you a quick way to test what you know about how government works.

**The work behind the pages**

- 32,729 sourced positions across 3,894 politicians.
- Budget data for 2,454 governments across all 50 states and DC.
- 27.6 million federal campaign-finance records totaling $7.6 billion. Each contribution traces back to its original FEC filing.

[The full briefing](https://empowered.vote/briefing) has the counts, sources, and a fuller explanation of what is live, what is in alpha, and what comes next.

**Where coverage is still thin**

We have at least one researched official in every state and coverage across all 50 congressional delegations, but state and local depth is uneven across the 51 jurisdictions we track — the 50 states plus DC:

- 10 have deep coverage: legislatures, counties, and cities.
- 5 are growing.
- The other 36 mostly have governors and statewide officers so far.

If you look up a city council member in one of those 36, you may not find them yet.

We are eight volunteers, and the platform is still in alpha. The next few months are about filling those gaps and finishing the 2026 candidate coverage. Our goal is to send one of these a month with a straightforward update on what changed and what still needs work.

**One ask this month, and it's not money: reply if you have a thought.** Tell me what you would want to see in these notes, or what confused you on the site. We actually do read these replies.

If you know someone who might find the tools useful, please forward this note. There is no pressure to do that, either.

Thanks for being here early.
— Chris *(and the rest of the volunteers)*
