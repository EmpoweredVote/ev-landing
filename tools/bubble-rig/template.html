Groupings are the five themes from presidential_quotes.md. Figures are the site's own leremy-rig, running the real read → lookup → hold → resume sequence; hovering a reader glances him up and pauses his bubble's timer, as on the homepage.

Two departures from production, both to keep the test readable: opening one reader closes any other (the homepage allows several at once), and the bubble is anchored to a shelf here rather than a note-card edge. Bubbles overlay whatever is above them — that is production behaviour, not a layout bug. Resize the window and measure again to see the narrow-viewport clamp.

A nineteenth quote used to be here: Jefferson's “difference of opinion leads to enquiry, and enquiry to truth.” It was cut on 4 August 2026, because he wrote it in a draft and then decided not to send it — a letter withheld is closer to something unpublished than to something said, and a Bobit saying it would claim he said it to someone. It was also the only quote in the pool never read on its own linked page, so with it gone every quote here has been read at its source. The full record is under Removed in presidential_quotes.md.
