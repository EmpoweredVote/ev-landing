# Empowered Compass — episode script

## L001
> Direction: a Bobit alone at a voting booth, a tall column of unfamiliar names beside it. `confused`.
Here's something that happens to almost everyone:  It's election day.  You get to your polling booth, work your way down your ballot, and somewhere past the top of it the names stop meaning anything.

## L002
> Direction: the same Bobit, shoulders dropping, the column still towering. `spent`.
You're not uninformed. You are out of time. There were forty races on that ballot and you had a lunch break.

## L003
> Direction: a single letter appears beside each name on the column — D, R, D, D, R.
So you do what the ballot makes easy. You look for the letter next to the name, and you let the letter decide.

## L004
> Direction: the letters stay; the Bobit looks at them, head tilted. `ponder`.
That letter is doing an enormous amount of work for one character.  And it's worth asking, how much does the letter actually earn?

## L005
> Direction: title card on Inform yellow.
This is Empowered Compass.

## L006
> Direction: two Bobits stand apart. A large box drops around each one, each box labelled with a letter.
A party label is a bundle of stances. Dozens of positions, packed together by somebody who is not you, and handed to you as a single choice.

## L007
> Direction: the two Bobits inside the same box turn away from each other.
Put two people in the same box and they will still disagree about plenty. The box doesn't promise otherwise.

## L008
> Direction: one Bobit from each box steps toward the other; they meet between the boxes.
And if you have two people in different boxes, they tend to agree about far more than either of them expects. The boxes just make that hard to see.

## L009
> Direction: the boxes fade out. Both Bobits remain, standing level with each other.
So the letter answers a question; just not the one you were asking.  It tells you whose team someone is on, not whether they would vote the way you would.

## L010
> Direction: one Bobit, `present`, with the phrase "start from you" appearing.
The Empowered Compass starts from the other end. Not with the parties and their bundles, but with you, and the handful of things you actually care about.

## L011
> Direction: the phrase "voter driven, not party driven" appears.
Voter driven, not party driven. That's the whole idea, putting what citizens want at the heart of our democracy.

## L012
> Direction: the words "vote-worthy issue" appear, defined underneath.
We call these things your vote-worthy issues. Not every opinion you hold. Just the few that would genuinely change who you vote for.

## L013
> Direction: a Bobit `showTo` beside the goal, which appears as a single line.
The goal is to turn your priorities into a picture. One you can read in a second and hold up against anybody asking for your vote.

## L014
> Direction: a Bobit `tap`, holding a phone, markers dropping one at a time as it answers.
You start by calibrating your compass.  Which is done through a quick questionnaire.

## L015
> Direction: the same Bobit, still tapping. A second marker drops.
It asks two different things.  First, what issues belong on your list at all?

## L016
> Direction: a third marker drops, further out than the others.
And second, for each topic, where do you actually land?  What does your solution look like?

## L017
> Direction: the Bobit lowers the phone, `present`. The markers stay on screen.
You choose which issues.  We show you how you how candidates feel on those issues.  And then you choose how you want to vote. 

## L018
> Direction: the markers rearrange into a loose ring, hinting at the shape to come.
What comes out the other side is not a score and not a label. It is a customized shape that represents you.

## L019
> Direction: `art` — a single labelled axis draws out from the centre. A Bobit `showTo` beside it.
Take one issue on its own.  Like Housing.  

## L020
> Direction: `art` — the answers appear as named stops along the axis, in order.
If that seems like a vote-worthy issue to you, then it becomes a spoke. A line running out from a centre point, with the realistic answers to it set out in order along the line.

## L021
> Direction: `art` — a dot slides from the centre out along the spoke and settles near the far end.
We sort common answers along a spectrum.  For instance with housing, at one end we have the government not intervening and letting the markets decide, and at the other end we have the government building low income housing.

## L022
> Direction: `art` — the dot holds on its stop, and the two ends of the ladder stay named.
You choose the answer that fits you best, or you write your own. Your dot sits on that answer. The spoke is not measuring how strongly you feel. It is recording which solution you want.

## L023
> Direction: `art` — the remaining spokes draw in, one after another, still empty.
Then do that for each issue on your list.

## L024
> Direction: `art` — a dot appears on each spoke in turn, at a different distance.
Each topic gets a spoke, and each spoke gets your dot describing your stance.

## L025
> Direction: `art` — the outline joins the dots, edge by edge, and closes.
Then join them up. The outline that connects your points is your compass.

## L026
> Direction: `art` — the filled shape holds. Two Bobits look at it. `present` and `showTo`.
And a shape is a thing a person can actually read. Not a number, not a percentage, not a side. A shape you recognise as yours.

## L027
> Direction: `art` — your shape holds, dimmer. Space opens beside it.
Once it's calibrated you can lay something else over it.

## L028
> Direction: `art` — a second outline in the accent colour fades in over the first.
Bring in a civic leader. Their positions get placed on the same spokes, at the same scale, and drawn as a second shape.

## L029
> Direction: `art` — the region where the two shapes overlap fills in.
Where the two shapes cover the same ground, you agree. That region is real agreement, on the issues you picked.

## L030
> Direction: `art` — one spoke where the viewer's shape reaches well past the leader's is highlighted.
And where your shapes don't align, you are looking at something specific. 

## L031
> Direction: `art` — a spoke the viewer left empty stays dark while both shapes hold.
The issues you never put on your list stay off the picture entirely. 

## L033
> Direction: the shapes hold. The phrase "you read it yourself" appears.
And notice what the tool does not do. It does not rank them for you, or pick for you, or tell you who your candidate is. It draws both shapes honestly and lets you look.

## L034
> Direction: `art` — the two shapes hold, dimmer. One of the leader's dots is picked out.
Which leaves the question that matters most. Where did their dot come from? Who decided that this is where this person stands?

## L035
> Direction: `art` — a card opens beside the shape, headed with the topic and the chosen answer.
Nobody guessed. Every stance we place on a spoke is researched, one person and one topic at a time. We pick the answer on the ladder that their record actually supports, and we write down why.

## L036
> Direction: `art` — numbered references appear under the card, each with its source name.
And we keep the receipts. Each one carries its references. Voting records. Bill texts. Legislature sites. The public record it came out of. You can open any of them yourself.

## L037
> Direction: `art` — the card dims and the three counts come up over it.
That is most of the work behind this tool. Tens of thousands of positions, across thousands of officials, with tens of thousands of references under them.

## L038
> Direction: cut to a few seconds of the real compass.empowered.vote screen.
And this one is not a sketch. The Empowered Compass is live today, at compass dot empowered dot vote.

## L039
> Direction: the real screen holds, then the Bobits return in front of it.
Calibrate once. After that, every election, you hold your shape up against whoever is asking, and you decide for yourself.

## L040
> Direction: two Bobits, `cheer`.  Text of 'compass.empowered.vote'
That's the Empowered Compass.  Now go draw your own.
