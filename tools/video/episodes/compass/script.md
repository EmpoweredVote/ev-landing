# Empowered Compass — episode script

## L001
> Direction: a Bobit alone at a voting booth, a tall column of unfamiliar names beside it. `confused`.
Here is a thing that happens to almost everyone. You get to the booth, you work your way down the ballot, and somewhere past the top of it the names stop meaning anything.

## L002
> Direction: the same Bobit, shoulders dropping, the column still towering. `spent`.
You are not uninformed. You are out of time. There were forty races on that ballot and you had a lunch break.

## L003
> Direction: a single letter appears beside each name on the column — D, R, D, D, R.
So you do what the ballot makes easy. You look for the letter next to the name, and you let the letter decide.

## L004
> Direction: the letters stay; the Bobit looks at them, head tilted. `ponder`.
That letter is doing an enormous amount of work for one character. It is worth asking how much of it the letter actually earns.

## L005
> Direction: title card on Inform yellow.
This is Empowered Compass.

## L006
> Direction: two Bobits stand apart. A large box drops around each one, each box labelled with a letter.
A party label is a bundle. Dozens of positions, packed together by somebody who is not you, and handed to you as a single choice.

## L007
> Direction: the two Bobits inside the same box turn away from each other.
Put two people in the same box and they will still disagree about plenty. The box does not promise otherwise. It never did.

## L008
> Direction: one Bobit from each box steps toward the other; they meet between the boxes.
And two people in different boxes agree about far more than either of them expects. The boxes just make that hard to see.

## L009
> Direction: the boxes fade out. Both Bobits remain, standing level with each other.
So the letter answers a question. Just not the one you were asking. It tells you whose team someone is on, not whether they would vote the way you would.

## L010
> Direction: one Bobit, `present`, with the phrase "start from you" appearing.
Empowered Compass starts from the other end. Not with the parties and their bundles, but with you, and the handful of things you actually vote on.

## L011
> Direction: the phrase "voter driven, not party driven" appears.
Voter driven, not party driven. That is the whole idea, and everything else in this tool is in service of it.

## L012
> Direction: the words "vote-worthy issue" appear, defined underneath.
We call those things your vote-worthy issues. Not every opinion you hold. The few that would genuinely change who you vote for.

## L013
> Direction: a Bobit `showTo` beside the goal, which appears as a single line.
The goal is to turn those priorities into a picture. One you can read in a second, and hold up against anybody asking for your vote.

## L014
> Direction: a Bobit `tap`, holding a phone, markers dropping one at a time as it answers.
You start by calibrating. That is a questionnaire, and it is the only part that asks anything of you.

## L015
> Direction: the same Bobit, still tapping. A second marker drops.
It asks two different things, and the difference matters. First, which issues belong on your list at all.

## L016
> Direction: a third marker drops, further out than the others.
And second, for each one, where you actually land, and how hard you hold it.

## L017
> Direction: the Bobit lowers the phone, `present`. The markers stay on screen.
You choose the issues. The tool does not hand you a fixed list and score you against it, because the list is the part that is personal.

## L018
> Direction: the markers rearrange into a loose ring, hinting at the shape to come.
What comes out the other side is not a score and not a label. It is yours, and it is a shape.

## L019
> Direction: `art` — a single labelled axis draws out from the centre. A Bobit `showTo` beside it.
Take one issue on its own. Housing, say.

## L020
> Direction: `art` — tick marks appear along the axis.
It becomes a spoke. A line running out from a centre point, with your feeling about that issue measured along it.

## L021
> Direction: `art` — a dot slides from the centre out along the spoke and settles near the far end.
Sit near the middle and you are telling the tool this one does not move you much. Sit far out and you are saying this issue decides your vote on its own.

## L022
> Direction: `art` — the dot holds. The caption "how strongly you feel" sits under the axis.
That is the entire mechanic. How far out you sit is how much it matters to you.

## L023
> Direction: `art` — the remaining spokes draw in, one after another, still empty.
Now do that for every issue you put on your list.

## L024
> Direction: `art` — a dot appears on each spoke in turn, at a different distance.
Each one gets a spoke, and each spoke gets your dot at whatever distance you chose.

## L025
> Direction: `art` — the outline joins the dots, edge by edge, and closes.
Then join them up. The outline that closes around those points is your compass.

## L026
> Direction: `art` — the filled shape holds. Two Bobits look at it. `present` and `showTo`.
And a shape is a thing a person can actually read. Not a number, not a percentage, not a side. A shape you recognise as yours.

## L027
> Direction: `art` — your shape holds, dimmer. Space opens beside it.
Which is the point, because now you can lay something else over it.

## L028
> Direction: `art` — a second outline in the accent colour fades in over the first.
Bring in a civic leader. Their positions get placed on the same spokes, at the same scale, and drawn as a second shape.

## L029
> Direction: `art` — the region where the two shapes overlap fills in.
Where the two shapes cover the same ground, you agree. That region is real agreement, on the issues you picked, at the strength you picked.

## L030
> Direction: `art` — one spoke where the viewer's shape reaches well past the leader's is highlighted.
And where your shape reaches out past theirs, you are looking at something specific. An issue you care about more than the person asking for your vote does.

## L031
> Direction: `art` — a spoke the viewer left empty stays dark while both shapes hold.
The issues you never put on your list stay off the picture entirely. Nobody gets credit, or blame, for a thing you did not ask about.

## L032
> Direction: two Bobits beside the overlaid shapes, `showTo` and `present`.
This is the part the letter beside the name cannot do. A label cannot tell you where someone sits on the four things you personally vote on.

## L033
> Direction: the shapes hold. The phrase "you read it yourself" appears.
And notice what the tool does not do. It does not rank them for you, or pick for you, or tell you who your candidate is. It draws both shapes honestly and lets you look.

## L034
> Direction: three blocks appear in turn — Inform yellow, Connect teal, Empower red.
Empowered Compass is one tool in the first of three pillars. Inform, then Connect, then Empower.

## L035
> Direction: the Inform block highlights; the other two dim.
Inform is the pillar for seeing clearly before you argue. Compass sits here with the other tools that answer "what is actually true, and what do I actually want."

## L036
> Direction: the formula appears: sFacts + sValues = sSolutions.
The whole platform runs on one formula. Shared facts, plus shared values, give you shared solutions.

## L037
> Direction: the "sValues" term highlights in the accent colour.
Compass is the shared-values half of that, made visible. You cannot find common ground with someone until both of you can see where you are standing.

## L038
> Direction: cut to a few seconds of the real compass.empowered.vote screen.
And this one is not a sketch. Empowered Compass is live today, at compass dot empowered dot vote.

## L039
> Direction: the real screen holds, then the Bobits return in front of it.
Calibrate once. After that, every election, you hold your shape up against whoever is asking, and you decide for yourself.

## L040
> Direction: two Bobits, `cheer`.
That is Empowered Compass. Go and draw your own.
